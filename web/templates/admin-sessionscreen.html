Host presets

The mainframes the selection screen and the connect page offer. Each preset can be for everyone, or only for the users, groups or roles it names — an operator is never shown a host they would be refused.

The bundled sample apps are here from the start, marked not offered: they are on this page and nowhere else until you Offer one, or give it an audience. Remove any you do not want and they stay removed.
